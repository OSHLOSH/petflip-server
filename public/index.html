How do I deposit pets?
Link your Roblox account using the "Link Account" button, then click "Deposit". Trade your pets to one of our bots and they'll appear in your inventory automatically.
How does Coinflip work?
Create a flip by selecting pets from your inventory and choosing Heads or Tails. Another player can join — winner takes all. Each side picks the opposite face of the coin.
How do withdrawals work?
Go to your inventory and click Withdraw. Select the pets you want to receive and join one of our bots. The bot will send the trade to you in-game.
Is PetFlip provably fair?
Yes! Every flip uses a cryptographically secure server seed combined with your client seed and a nonce. You can verify any result on the Provably Fair page.
What pets are accepted?
We accept Huge, Titanic, and Gargantuan rarity pets from Pet Simulator 99. Values are updated regularly based on current market prices.

How it Works

Each game uses a server seed (pre-committed as a hash), your client seed, and a nonce. The roll is derived from HMAC-SHA256(serverSeed, clientSeed:nonce). After the game, the server seed is revealed so you can independently verify the outcome.

FAQ

Common questions about PetFlip

What is PetFlip?
PetFlip is a peer-to-peer PS99 trading platform. Wager your in-game pets and gems against other players in Coinflip, Jackpot, and Upgrader games. All outcomes are provably fair using cryptographic verification.
How do I deposit pets?
Link your Roblox account first, then click Deposit in the sidebar. You'll join a trade with one of our verified PS99 bots. The bot will accept your trade and your pets appear in your inventory instantly.
How do I link my account?
Click "Link Account" in the top bar. Enter your Roblox username and you'll receive a short phrase to say in PS99 chat. Our bot verifies it and links your account automatically — it takes under a minute.
What are the gem tiers?
Pets are ranked: Gargantuan > Titanic > Huge. Values are measured in diamonds based on current PS99 market rates and are updated regularly by the admin.
What is the house edge?
Coinflip: 0%. Jackpot: 2% rake on the pot. Upgrader: 4% applied to expected value. All outcomes are verifiable — no hidden edge.
Can I be scammed?
PetFlip handles all transfers through verified PS99 bot accounts. You never trade directly with another player, eliminating scam risk entirely. Withdrawals are processed by our bots within minutes.
Is PetFlip provably fair?
Yes. Every game uses HMAC-SHA256 combining a server seed, your client seed, and a nonce. The server seed hash is committed before each game and revealed after. Use the Provably Fair page to verify any result independently.

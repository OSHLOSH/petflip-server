FAQ
Common questions about PetFlip
What is PetFlip?
PetFlip is a peer-to-peer PS99 trading platform. You can wager your in-game pets and gems against other players in Coinflip, Jackpot, Upgrader, and Mines games. All outcomes are provably fair.
How do I link my PS99 account?
Click "Link Account" in the top bar. You will receive a 6-word phrase to say in the PS99 game chat. Our bot verifies it and links your account automatically.
What are the gem tiers?
Pets are ranked: Gargantuan, Titanic, Huge, Exclusive, Legendary, Rare. Values are measured in diamonds based on current PS99 market rates.
Is PetFlip provably fair?
Yes. Every game uses HMAC-SHA256 combining a server seed, your client seed, and a nonce. The server seed hash is committed before each game and revealed after, so you can independently verify every result.
What is the house edge?
Coinflip: 0%. Jackpot: 2% rake on the pot. Upgrader: 4% applied to expected value. Mines: ~4% built into the multiplier curve.
Can I be scammed?
PetFlip handles all transfers through our verified PS99 bot accounts. You never trade directly with another player, eliminating the risk of scam trades.

How it works

Everyone throws pets into one pot. Your chance to win equals your share of the pot value. Once 2+ players are in, a 20s timer starts — then the wheel picks one winner who takes everything.

ℹ️No other players in the pot? Call the house bot — it adds pets matching your entry value.

How results are generated

Before every game a random server seed is created and its SHA-256 hash is shown to you. The result is SHA256(serverSeed:clientSeed:nonce) — the first 13 hex characters turned into a number between 0 and 1. After the game the server seed is revealed, so you can check the hash matches and recompute the roll yourself. You can change your client seed from your profile.

Coinflip: roll < 0.5 = Heads, otherwise Tails. Jackpot: roll × pot value picks the winning ticket. Upgrader: roll < win chance = success.

Verify a roll

Paste seeds from any game in History, or click “Verify” on a history row.
How do I deposit?

Click Deposit, join one of our bots' servers in Pet Simulator 99, and send it a trade with the pets or gems you want to deposit. Once the trade completes they show up in your inventory.

How do I withdraw?

Click Withdraw, select the items you want, confirm, then join the bot's server. The bot will send you a trade with your items — accept it in-game.

How are pet values decided?

Every pet has a fixed value in our catalogue (click “Check Pet Values” in the deposit window). Values are updated as the market moves.

What is “Call Bot”?

If nobody joins your coinflip or the jackpot, the house bot can join with pets matching your value. Odds are exactly the same as against a player.

Beware of impersonators

Only trade with the bot usernames shown in the Deposit/Withdraw window. Staff will never DM you first asking for pets.

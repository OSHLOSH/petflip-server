FAQ
Deposits, withdrawals & more

Deposits

How do I deposit pets?
Link your Roblox account via the Link button, then open the Deposit panel and select pets from your inventory. A bot will join your game and trade for them.
How long do deposits take?
Usually under 2 minutes. The bot needs to join your server — make sure your game is public and you're in a Pet Simulator 99 server.
What pets can I deposit?
All tradeable pets from Pet Simulator 99 are accepted. The value is calculated from the latest market rates.

Withdrawals

How do I withdraw my pets?
Go to your inventory, select the pets you want to withdraw, and confirm. A bot will trade them to you in-game.
Why is my withdrawal pending?
Withdrawals are processed in order. If a bot isn't available immediately, your withdrawal will queue and process automatically.

Games

Is PetFlip provably fair?
Yes! Every result is generated using a combination of your client seed, our server seed, and a nonce. You can verify any result on the Provably Fair page.
What is the house edge?
PetFlip takes a small percentage from each game. This is displayed before you confirm any bet.
